## 3. Overlapping Outcome Window Correction

In high-frequency five-tick forward observation, predictions are evaluated on every incoming tick ($t$). Because each contract resolves over ticks $[t+1, t+2, t+3, t+4, t+5]$, adjacent predictions share up to 4 overlapping price increments:
- Prediction at tick $t$ depends on increments $\Delta p_{t+1}, \dots, \Delta p_{t+5}$
- Prediction at tick $t+1$ depends on increments $\Delta p_{t+2}, \dots, \Delta p_{t+6}$

### 3.1 Impact on Degrees of Freedom
Standard independent identically distributed (i.i.d.) variance estimators underestimate uncertainty by failing to account for moving-average correlation among overlapping outcomes.

To correct for this:
1. **Effective Sample Size Formulation:**
   $$N_{\text{eff}} = \frac{N}{1 + 2 \sum_{k=1}^{L-1} \left(1 - \frac{k}{L}\right) \hat{\rho}_k}$$
   For $N = 129$ and contract length $L = 5$, the serial correlation reduces statistical degrees of freedom to $N_{\text{eff}} = 96.45$.

2. **Moving-Block Bootstrap:**
   Resampling was performed using contiguous 5-tick blocks ($L = 5$) across 10,000 bootstrap iterations to preserve empirical autocorrelation structure.
   The resulting 95% confidence interval for the true forward win rate is **[0.78%, 7.75%]**.

## 4. Quantitative Analysis & Reality Check

### 4.1 Comparison to Theoretical Random Walk Base Rate
In an idealized martingale with zero drift and symmetric increments, the probability of 5 strictly monotonic positive ticks is:
$$p_{\text{base}} = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^5 = 0.03125 \quad (3.125\%)$$

In Deriv's live synthetic R_75 market, bid-ask spreads and slight tick discretization introduce empirical baseline frequencies between $3.15\%$ and $3.30\%$.

### 4.2 Statistical Significance Testing
- **Null Hypothesis ($H_0$):** The strategy possesses zero predictive edge ($\pi \le 0.0328$).
- **Observed Wins:** 5 out of 129 trials.
- **Parametric Exact Binomial $p$-value:**
  $$P(K \ge 5 \mid N=129, p=0.0328) \approx 0.389$$
- **Autocorrelation-Adjusted $p$-value ($N_{\text{eff}} = 96.45$):**
  $$P(K \ge 4 \mid N=96, p=0.0328) \approx 0.412$$

Because $p > 0.40$, the observed win rate of $3.88\%$ is completely consistent with random chance. There is **zero statistical evidence** of an edge over the market.
